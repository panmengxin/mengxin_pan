<h2 class="section-title">Research Focus</h2>

<p>My research combines advanced artificial intelligence techniques with climate dynamics to address real-world problems. The work has two interconnected objectives: first, advancing fundamental insight into climate and weather extremes, with specific interests in atmospheric rivers and the Amazon's expanding dry-hot season; second, translating scientific insight into practical applications that address climate-change challenges.</p>
