    <h2 style="font-size: clamp(20px, 4vw, 24px); color: #333; margin: 20px 0 10px; padding-bottom: 8px; border-bottom: 2px solid #75bbfd;">Research &amp; Publications</h2>

    <p style="font-size: clamp(16px, 3vw, 18px); line-height: 1.5; margin-bottom: 0px; text-indent: 2em; text-align: justify;">My research combines advanced artificial intelligence techniques with climate dynamics to address real-world problems, with two interconnected objectives. First, <strong>advancing fundamental insight into climate and weather extremes</strong>, with specific interests in <strong>atmospheric rivers</strong> and the Amazon's expanding dry-hot season and how they change in a warming climate. Second, <strong>translating scientific insight into real-world applications that address climate-change challenges</strong>.</p>

    <h3 style="font-size: clamp(18px, 3vw, 20px); color: #444; margin: 15px 0 5px;">1. Extreme weather under climate change</h3>

    <p style="font-size: clamp(16px, 3vw, 18px); line-height: 1.5; margin-bottom: 5px; text-indent: 2em; text-align: justify;"><strong>Atmospheric rivers (ARs)</strong> — "the rivers in the sky" — are long, narrow, transient corridors of intense moisture transport in the atmosphere. They supply essential moisture to terrestrial ecosystems and human water resources, while the landfall of extreme ARs can also bring life-threatening weather.</p>

    <div style="display: flex; justify-content: center; gap: 10px; flex-wrap: wrap; margin: 10px 0;">
        <img src="/assets/AR_example.JPEG"
             alt="Atmospheric river over the Pacific Ocean"

    </div>

    <p style="font-size: clamp(16px, 3vw, 18px); line-height: 1.5; margin: 10px 0; text-indent: 2em; text-align: justify;">Using ARs as the bridge, I study <strong>how large-scale climate variability modulates mid-latitude extreme weather</strong> in a warming climate. With machine-learning algorithms, I develop AR detection methods, redefine the annual cycle of East Asian ARs, characterize the diverse propagation behavior of cross-Pacific ARs, and examine whether ARs have become more frequent under ongoing climate change and the underlying mechanisms.</p>

    <h3 style="font-size: clamp(18px, 3vw, 20px); color: #444; margin: 15px 0 5px;">2. Climate change and society</h3>

    <p style="font-size: clamp(16px, 3vw, 18px); line-height: 1.5; margin: 5px 0; text-indent: 2em; text-align: justify;">Starting from climate dynamics, I lead <strong>interdisciplinary studies of climate impacts in a warming world</strong> that provide holistic understanding and practical climate solutions. With <strong>global-health</strong> collaborators, I explore the overlooked role of tropical oceans in vector-borne disease prediction. With <strong>civil-engineering</strong> collaborators, I investigate the resilience of bridges to extreme temperatures under global warming.</p>
