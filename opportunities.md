<p class="research-note">The Climate and Weather Extremes Lab is recruiting <strong>Master's and Ph.D. students</strong> for <strong>Fall 2026 or Spring/Fall 2027</strong>, with comprehensive funding covering tuition and living expenses in Vancouver, Canada.</p>

<h2 class="section-title">Research Theme</h2>

<p>The broad research theme is <strong>applied data science in climate science</strong>. Students from climate-science and data-science backgrounds are welcome, including climate dynamics, atmospheric science, oceanography, physics, statistics, global health, social science, and civil engineering. Interdisciplinary applicants interested in climate-change impacts and practical climate solutions are especially encouraged to get in touch.</p>

<h2 class="section-title">Possible Research Directions</h2>

<ul class="compact-list">
  <li>Atmospheric rivers, extreme precipitation, and changing storm-track behavior.</li>
  <li>Machine-learning methods for climate and weather extremes.</li>
  <li>Climate-change impacts on health, infrastructure, and society.</li>
  <li>Climate-risk information that connects physical understanding with decision-making.</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="section-title">Qualifications</h2>

<ul class="compact-list">
  <li>Bachelor's degree for Master's applicants or Master's degree for Ph.D. applicants.</li>
  <li>Strong interest in applied data science in climate science.</li>
  <li>Programming and data-analysis proficiency are preferred.</li>
  <li>Strong academic performance.</li>
  <li>Demonstrated self-motivation, independence, and critical-thinking ability.</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="section-title">How To Apply</h2>

<ul class="compact-list">
  <li>Please send your <strong>CV, personal/research statement, and academic transcripts</strong> to <a href="mailto:mengxinp@sfu.ca">mengxinp@sfu.ca</a>.</li>
  <li>I prefer to admit students with prior contact, so early inquiries are strongly encouraged.</li>
  <li>English-proficiency scores and publications are not required for an initial inquiry.</li>
  <li>Please email me as early as possible if you are interested.</li>
</ul>

<p>See the <a href="https://www.sfu.ca/geography/prospective-students/prospective-graduate.html" target="_blank">SFU Geography graduate application procedure</a> for more information.</p>
