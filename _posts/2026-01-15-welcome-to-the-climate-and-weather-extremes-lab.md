The Climate and Weather Extremes Lab at Simon Fraser University studies atmospheric rivers, climate extremes, and applied data science in climate science.

The lab connects physical understanding of climate and weather extremes with practical applications for climate-change challenges. Current directions include atmospheric rivers, large-scale climate variability, tropical-ocean links to malaria prediction, and infrastructure risks under extreme heat.
