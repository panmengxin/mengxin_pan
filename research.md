<p>My research combines advanced artificial intelligence techniques with climate dynamics to address real-world problems. The work advances physical understanding of climate and weather extremes, then translates that understanding into applications that can support climate-change adaptation.</p>

<h2 id="extreme-weather-under-climate-change" class="section-title">Extreme Weather Under Climate Change</h2>

<p><strong>Atmospheric rivers (ARs)</strong> - "the rivers in the sky" - are long, narrow, transient corridors of intense moisture transport in the atmosphere. They supply essential moisture to terrestrial ecosystems and human water resources, while landfalling extreme ARs can also bring life-threatening weather.</p>

<div class="media-grid media-grid--single">
  <img src="{{ '/assets/AR_example.JPEG' | relative_url }}" alt="Atmospheric river over the Pacific Ocean">

<p>Using ARs as a bridge between climate dynamics and high-impact weather, I study how large-scale climate variability modulates mid-latitude extremes in a warming climate. With machine-learning algorithms, I develop AR detection methods, redefine the annual cycle of East Asian ARs, characterize the diverse propagation behavior of cross-Pacific ARs, and examine whether ARs have become more frequent under ongoing climate change and why.</p>

<h2 id="climate-change-and-society" class="section-title">Climate Change And Society</h2>

<p>Starting from climate dynamics, I lead interdisciplinary studies of climate impacts in a warming world that provide holistic understanding and practical climate solutions. With global-health collaborators, I explore the overlooked role of tropical oceans in vector-borne disease prediction. With civil-engineering collaborators, I investigate the resilience of bridges to extreme temperatures under global warming.</p>

<h2 class="section-title">Methods And Data</h2>

<p>The lab uses climate dynamics, machine learning, remote sensing, reanalysis data, statistical modeling, and interdisciplinary collaboration. Students and collaborators work across physical climate science, data science, global health, civil engineering, and climate-risk applications.</p>
